# When the menu is empty, consult

Proposed by the sovereign: *assume we have nothing in the menu. We should move to consultations,
and the result of consultation should be a plan, with the menu as a side effect.*

Half of it is already the recorded contract for `deliberation:Consulting`, arrived at from the
other end — [a-plan-is-a-path-of-graph-diffs](/decisions/a-plan-is-a-path-of-graph-diffs.md) says
the model's whole output is *an affordance AS PREMISES, held to shapes, adopted, and then
exploited by the reflex free forever*. The sovereign's contribution is the trigger and the
consequence: being STUCK is when to ask, and the menu follows rather than being written.

## The trigger is a number that already exists

An agent whose menu holds nothing that would move a goal reports it: `unactionable`, counted per
tick since the goals panel landed. It was added so a drowning plant would not graph like a
thirsty one, and it turns out to be exactly the condition under which asking something else is
worth the cost. The metric that reports the problem is the hinge to the escalation, which nobody
planned and which is the usual sign the state was worth naming.

Two conditions look alike from outside and are not: NOTHING ON THE MENU (no lever exists at all)
and NOTHING THAT HELPS (levers exist and none improves the world). The first is a question about
the world — is there a lever I do not know about — and is what consultation is for. The second is
already answered honestly and needs no model: my doses are too coarse, or the plant is drowning
and no lever lowers moisture.

## The correction: premises, not steps

**A consulted plan must arrive as premises.** If a model returns STEPS, it is authoring acts, and
*a plan is a path through the affordance graph, never arbitrary surgery an agent imagines onto
the world* stops being true. That is the self-satisfaction loophole arriving on the MEANS side
rather than the ends side: an agent that cannot author its own desires but can author its own
actions has given away the same guarantee through a different door.

If it returns premises — *this valve draws from a source that also feeds the pot*, *there is a
venue at X you may bid in* — then they are held to shapes, adopted, and **the row appears because
its premise holds.** Nothing writes a row, because rows are not stored: premises are, and the
menu is what they imply. The plan is then derived by the ordinary planner, simulated like any
other, and the sovereign's "menu as a side effect" is exactly right — with the plan a side effect
too.

## What you pay for once, you keep

A model call is expensive and a premise is durable. An adopted premise persists as long as it
holds, so the row it implies persists with it and the reflex exploits it for nothing thereafter.
That is why this belongs at the moment of being stuck rather than in the loop: the cost is paid
once per discovery, not once per tick.

It also means a consultation's value compounds in the right direction. The first agent to learn
that a hose reaches the far bed enriches a menu; every later decision that uses it is free.

## Two guards, both already built

**Ratification.** A premise is a claim about the world, and a model inventing one is a model
inventing a hose that is not there. The
[consulted-at-the-edge](/decisions/the-model-is-consulted-at-the-edge-of-knowledge.md) split applies unchanged: a PRESENT
sovereign ratifies, an ABSENT sovereign means private adoption plus review.

**The immune response, which is why private adoption is survivable.** An invented affordance gets
USED — and then the expected effect does not appear, the expectation goes unmet, and
`intention:suspectAfter` marks the affordance suspect after N consecutive failures. False
knowledge is detected by acting on it. That machinery was built for a device that lies about what
it dispensed; a model that invents a lever is the same failure with a different author, and the
same detector catches it.

So the risk of a consulted premise is bounded by exactly the thing this project already does
about every claim it cannot verify in advance: act, watch, and stop trusting what keeps failing.

# Seams left open

- **What a premise may SAY.** The same question as *what a runtime-authored shape may contain* —
  a profile exists in outline and not in force. A premise that adds a valve to the world graph is
  a very different thing from one that names a venue the ACL already admits, and nothing yet
  distinguishes them.
- **When to consult, and how often.** A model call per stuck goal per tick would be ruinous, and
  patience is the obvious bound — the keeper already holds one — but nobody has decided whether
  being stuck twice about the same goal is worth asking twice.
- **Whether a consulted premise is public or private.** It is a claim about the WORLD, which
  argues public; it was not ratified, which argues private until it is. The visibility axis makes
  both sayable and neither is chosen here.
- **What happens to a premise that is true and useless.** Suspicion catches a lever that fails.
  A lever that works and never helps — a fan for a plant that is never too hot — is neither false
  nor worth keeping, and nothing prunes it.

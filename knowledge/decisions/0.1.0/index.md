# Decisions of Agent 0.1.0

The records whose premises are the retired kernel — its packages, its layers, its capabilities,
its market of signed claims. Each is superseded, most by
[agent-0-2-0-replaced-the-kernel](/decisions/agent-0-2-0-replaced-the-kernel.md), and kept so that
what was argued, and what was refused, still resolves. Nothing here binds the code; the current
records are in [decisions](/decisions/index.md).

# Principles that cut across everything

* [the-mind-is-not-a-package](/decisions/0.1.0/the-mind-is-not-a-package.md) - SUPERSEDED IN PART — wanting, committing and deciding are the kernel's; the monolith seam is overturned by a-layer-is-a-distribution.
* [self-is-bdi-and-wiring-is-the-packages](/decisions/0.1.0/self-is-bdi-and-wiring-is-the-packages.md) - `Self` keeps id, capabilities and whom it acts for; sensors, actuators and venues are loaded by their packages' own `wiring.py`.
* [english-vs-formal](/decisions/0.1.0/english-vs-formal.md) - English for what is contested, RDF and SHACL for what is trusted.

# Trust, identity and isolation

* [trust-boundary](/decisions/0.1.0/trust-boundary.md) - Agents cite but never author facts, mint currency, or actuate. Three privileged powers stay in trusted infrastructure.
* [thin-trusted-infra](/decisions/0.1.0/thin-trusted-infra.md) - Relax those powers toward public functions and signed artifacts; only the currency ledger is irreducibly trusted.
* [the-sovereign-may-ask](/decisions/0.1.0/the-sovereign-may-ask.md) - One SPARQL question to one running agent over the world's bus. Disclosure, not access; read-only by construction.
* [belief-base-isolation](/decisions/0.1.0/belief-base-isolation.md) - SUPERSEDED — privacy by per-graph ACLs. The intent is stronger now; there is no shared store to be let into.
* [two-store-beliefs](/decisions/0.1.0/two-store-beliefs.md) - SUPERSEDED IN PART — a series store and a graph store, joined by URI and never federated. The split holds; Fuseki is gone.

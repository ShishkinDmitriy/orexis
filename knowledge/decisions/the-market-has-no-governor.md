# The market has no governor: deficit opens rounds, structure names the convener

Asked by the sovereign in three passes, each sharper: is the host defined by deficit? Could a
consumer host for several suppliers, and is that known before start? And finally, stripped of
every implementation: *"deficit is a state, and this state can change — so we can only define
the market, but don't know who rules it."* The answer turned out to be that you always know,
because the question decomposes.

## Ruling is commitment, and commitment never comes from state

A mechanism only works if it is fixed before play: bidders shape strategies against the
matching rule, so whoever operates it must be unable — or have no incentive — to bend it
mid-game. That power to commit has exactly two sources, **structure** (the position nobody can
defect around) and **neutrality** (no position at all), and today's deficit is neither. What
deficit governs is **activity** inside a standing mechanism: whether a round is worth opening
— the LOW band already does exactly this — who is eager, what clears, who is rationed.
Scarcity expresses itself as a price, never as a gavel.

If rulership followed measured deficit, the rules of the game would become a move in the game:
participants would invest in moving the *measurement* rather than in trading, which is
mechanism design's one unforgivable sin. So the split is
[control-the-derivative](/decisions/control-the-derivative-not-the-value.md) worn by the
market: state modulates activity, never authority. Persistent state — a market inverted for a
season, not a Tuesday — *argues* for a change of structure, to the party entitled to make one:
the host rotates with topology, exactly as
[standalone-clearing](/decisions/standalone-clearing.md) recorded, and topology changes by
amendment.

The taxonomy this principle generates: one owner facing many wanters convenes, because
structure makes its commitment credible — there is nowhere else to defect to. Many facing one
is the mirror, a procurement. And N facing M is the real question: with competition on both
sides **no participant has commitment power**, so the convener role must either become an
institution or dissolve.

## The decision: no institution — dealers, and bilateral dealing at the floor

Mature markets resolve N-to-M three ways: a neutral exchange running a double auction, a
dealer, or pairwise bargaining. The sovereign refused the first, and the refusal is
consistent rather than arbitrary. A stake-free exchange is a new trusted party holding power
over **allocation** — the exact power [trust-boundary](/decisions/trust-boundary.md) keeps out
of reach and [thin-trusted-infra](/decisions/thin-trusted-infra.md) spent its whole trajectory
shrinking. Clearing stayed thin precisely by *validating* and never allocating
([clearing-as-validator](/decisions/clearing-as-validator.md)); an exchange allocates, so it
re-fattens the middle. And a participant-shaped thing with no stake contradicts what this
project knows about agents: an agent advancing nobody's interest wants nothing
([desire-is-deduced-from-the-ranges-the-world-states](/decisions/desire-is-deduced-from-the-ranges-the-world-states.md))
— a stake-free host is not an agent, it is infrastructure wearing an agent's clothes.

So N-to-M clears through:

- **The dealer** — an intermediary *participant*: buys from the N, sells to the M, holds
  inventory, earns the spread. No collective mechanism needs ruling, because there is none —
  just a 1-to-M and an N-to-1 market glued together through one party's stock. This is how
  most thick two-sided markets actually clear, and the shipped supplier becomes one the day
  the city mains exists: buy upstream, sell downstream, two derived venues, the depth-2 plan
  [a-market-arises-where-want-meets-supply](/decisions/a-market-arises-where-want-meets-supply.md)
  already names.
- **Bilateral dealing** — the floor for the thin market: pairwise, no venue, legitimate when
  neither an auction nor a dealer would earn its keep.

## Fairness without a referee

The sovereign's condition on accepting this: avoid unfairness. It is held by four things
already present, all of them visibility and entry, none of them a power to referee a trade:

1. **Entry is one triple.** A venue is derived from stock plus consent — whoever holds a
   source and states `matchesBy` has opened shop. A dealer's spread is disciplined by the next
   dealer, and the next dealer costs a ratification, not a license.
2. **The rules precede play, in public.** The matching capability is public and announced in
   the offer ([bid-matching-is-a-capability](/decisions/bid-matching-is-a-capability.md)):
   nobody bids against terms it has not seen, and changing terms is a visible amendment, never
   a mid-round move.
3. **The ledger sees the spread.** Settlement speaks REA
   ([settlement-speaks-rea](/decisions/settlement-speaks-rea.md)): bought-at and sold-at are
   signed events, so a dealer's margin is a subtraction the sovereign can perform
   ([the-sovereign-may-ask](/decisions/the-sovereign-may-ask.md) reaches the beliefs, the
   ledger reaches the trades). Extraction is not forbidden; it is attributable — the same
   posture [the-ladder-of-means](/decisions/the-ladder-of-means.md) takes toward theft:
   evidence-producing, not merely hard.
4. **The quiet round already clears at cost.**
   [Uniform price dissolves the uncontested round](/decisions/uniform-price-dissolves-the-uncontested-round.md),
   so a dealer facing no contention cannot stage scarcity theatre.

Fairness here is a property of the market's transparency and contestability, not a power held
by anyone.

## The dealer's claim stays its own, because the stock is what decouples

The sovereign's last point: *"the dealer buys just to resell, so the claim should be granted
to another agent."* True of one kind of dealer, and not of the one this architecture is about
to grow — the distinction is worth the record:

- **The inventory dealer** wins upstream and redeems the claim *itself*: the mains' valve
  doses the dealer's own barrel. Downstream, its own venue mints fresh claims against its own
  stock. No claim ever changes hands, because the vessel decouples the buy side from the sell
  side — and that decoupling is what inventory *is*. The two markets never learn of each
  other; the plan connecting them lives in one agent's head, priced by its spread.
- **The pass-through dealer** holds no vessel, so the good must flow from the source straight
  to the final buyer — and then the winning claim must name a beneficiary who is not the
  winner. That is endorsement: clearing-signed like every claim, verified at the actuator like
  every command, and a real mechanism this project has not built, because nothing planned
  needs it.

## Can the notary itself be an agent? Asked immediately: "Notarius"

Split clearing by what it does, because the two halves answer differently. **Validating** — the
predicate over signed orders that [clearing-as-validator](/decisions/clearing-as-validator.md)
describes — is deterministic, public and stake-free, and *that* can be carried by a society
member: a Notarius, one container, one principal on the bus, a capability whose premise is
appointment — the sovereign hands it the co-signing key at genesis, exactly as loyalty is
installed in a bounded device at flash time. It is trustworthy not because its process is
special but because its work is **checkable**: it may sign only what the public predicate
validates, so an invalid co-signature is not a risk to be prevented but evidence that convicts
— self-incriminating, the way [the-ladder-of-means](/decisions/the-ladder-of-means.md) makes
theft evidence-producing rather than impossible. A Notarius is verification pushed into a
bounded *process*, the software mirror of the pump firmware verifying claims at the edge —
which is [thin-trusted-infra](/decisions/thin-trusted-infra.md)'s whole trajectory, continued.

Three disqualifiers keep it honest-shaped, and each is the exchange-refusal again in
miniature: **no stake** in any good its markets trade (an agent with desires about the lot
cannot notarise it); **no discretion** (it signs iff the predicate holds — a notary free to
decline a valid trade is an allocator again); **code in this repository** (the bounded-device
posture: loyalty auditable because the firmware is ours). Whether such a thing is an "agent"
is the [a-panel-is-a-sensor](/decisions/a-panel-is-a-sensor-not-an-agent.md) question one
floor up: agent by deployment shape — container, credential, derived capability — and not by
BDI, because it wants nothing, and saying so out loud is what keeps it legitimate.

What stays out of reach is the **ledger**. Double-spend prevention needs one serialiser;
appointing a single Notarius at a time preserves that by construction, several with equal keys
would break it without any of them misbehaving, and doing better than one is consensus — a
different project. So: Notarius yes, as the appointed, accountable co-signer; the ledger
remains the one irreducible trusted thing, exactly where thin-trusted-infra left it. What
cashing in the notary's self-incriminating evidence requires is revocation, which is already
the open flank ([#28](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/agora/issues/28),
[#29](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/agora/issues/29)).

# Seams left open

- **Claim endorsement** — a claim whose beneficiary differs from its winner. Trigger: the
  first world whose plumbing runs a source's valve to a stranger's pot with no dealer vessel
  between. Its signature chain is designed, settled when the sovereign asked whether the
  dealer should sign: FOUR signatures, each answering a different question. Host and clearing
  over the claim (exists) — the water was won. THE DEALER over the endorsement (jti +
  beneficiary) — necessary because redirecting a claim's benefit is disposing of the dealer's
  property, and on a bus possession of the bytes is nothing: every subscriber saw them; the
  keys and roster exist since #144, so this is one more signed statement. CLEARING over the
  retarget — the one nobody asks for until it bites: the original validation was
  per-beneficiary (rot headroom is a fact about whose pot the water lands in), so retargeting
  re-opens a question clearing already answered, and an uncountersigned endorsement is a hole
  in the constitution exactly one endorsement wide. The beneficiary over its presentation
  (exists) — which also settles consent: a dose is irreversible, but only the presenter
  triggers the pour, and presentation already waits for the beneficiary's live watch. The jti
  stays single-use throughout: endorsement retargets paper and never mints it, so double-spend
  stays the ledger's — the one irreducible trusted thing, as above.
- **Two dealers on one good** — the derivation already grants a venue to any stock plus
  consent, but no world yet demonstrates competing dealers, and the chooser this forces — a
  bidder facing two venues on one property — is the same cluster as the venue-denomination tie
  ([#198](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/agora/issues/198)).
- **The bilateral floor** — no vocabulary yet says "this pair trades without a venue";
  below-dealer thinness stays undescribed until a world is that thin.
- **The Notarius appointment** — clearing's validating half as a society member: an
  appointment fact in the world, a capability derived from it, the co-signing key mounted the
  way any principal's credential is. Nothing blocks it architecturally, but its accountability
  only has teeth once revocation exists, so the trigger is #28/#29 landing — before that, an
  agent-shaped notary is a key that cannot be taken back.

Amends [standalone-clearing](/decisions/standalone-clearing.md): its N-to-N row read "a
stake-free exchange hosts (order book)". The seam stays open — clearing remains
topology-invariant and that is untouched — but the resolution sketched there is refused;
dealers and bilateral dealing are the path.

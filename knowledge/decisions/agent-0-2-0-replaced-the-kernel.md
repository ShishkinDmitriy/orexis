# What was done

0.1.0 was a kernel of three layer packages, capabilities granted by premises, a choir of extension
points, a keeper walking an intention ledger and a market of signed claims across some twenty-five
distributions. Agent 0.2.0 was built beside it in `agent/`: one store whose catalogue says what
every graph is, belief revision by SHACL rules, sensing and prediction as packages, a planner that
searches possible worlds in an imaginarium, an executor holding the world to each step's
prediction, speech between agents as documents, and domains of files where 0.1.0 had capability
packages. It became what `orexis-agent` and the image run on 2026-09-25, and the old kernel, its
assembly, its packages and its suite were deleted on 2026-09-26.

# What was refused

- **Evolving 0.1.0 in place.** Its shapes were load-bearing in the wrong places: a mind granted
  as a capability, three layers as distributions, a store per modality. Each was argued down in a
  record of its own, and changing all of them in the running tree would have been a rewrite
  with the old suite pinning every intermediate state. A second tree could be held to its own
  suite from the first commit.
- **Keeping both.** Two kernels reading one vocabulary meant every term had two readers and every
  record two meanings. What 0.2.0 needed of a 0.1.0 package was moved into a domain or a package
  and changed there — amended, not copied — and once the market had moved too, nothing was left
  that only 0.1.0 did.

# What it leaves

The 0.1.0 vocabulary is kept under `tests/fixtures/retired/` so that the records still resolve;
nothing loads it. The records whose premises were 0.1.0's are filed under
[decisions/0.1.0](/decisions/0.1.0/index.md), superseded by this one unless something more specific
already superseded them.

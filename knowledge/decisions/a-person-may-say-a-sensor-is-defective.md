# The question

The terrace's battery channel was marked defective by a line in `world/terrace/world.ttl` (#939): sensing's
rules carry the defect onto each percept and conclude no reading of it. The sovereign asked to do the same from
the chat - "this sensor shows strange values" - without editing a file. But sensing's revision reads a percept
beside what the world states, the common beliefs, and nothing the agent derived
([a-transition-changes-the-state-and-an-inference-only-concludes](/decisions/a-transition-changes-the-state-and-an-inference-only-concludes.md)),
and a person's statement is kept as the agent's own and believed by an
[extraction](/domain/speech/extraction.md) into a belief of the agent's own. The word arrives and no rule reads it.

# What was decided

**Sensing ships a sayable and its extraction** (`agent/sensing/sayables.ttl`): a `sensing:DefectReport`, the
sensor and why, its why left out to say the sensor is sound again. It is offered only in a chat that
`speech:allows` it ([chat](/domain/speech/chat.md)), which is who may say it: the sovereign's chat allows every
sayable, and a chat with a stranger none.

**The extraction names the kind of belief it makes, `speech:believedAs`, and sensing reads that kind beside
the world.** The defect graph is a belief graph of the agent's own, holding `sensing:defect` of the sensor as
the world would state it; sensing hands belief's revision its kind to read beside the common beliefs, and the
rules that read the world's defect read it unchanged. The rule's text still says nothing of the graph it
writes; the extraction's node says what its conclusions are, as a rule's says its order.

**The report is keyed by its sensor, `speech:keyedBy`**, so its statement is named by the sensor, and said
again it replaces the one before - the one way a statement's belief goes, since an extraction reads one
statement alone and deletes nothing. Said with no why, the extraction concludes nothing and the defect graph
goes with what it was derived from.

It reaches the next percept: one revised already keeps what it was revised with, as a world's edit does until
the next reading.

# What was refused

- **Revision reading every belief extracted of a statement.** #953 refused it: revision would read one
  peer's extracted word beside another's, and a rule meant for the world's documents would be met by what a
  peer said. A kind named by one extraction is read where a reader asks for it, and nowhere else.
- **The model proposing an edit of the world's documents**, which the sovereign or an operator applies. The
  world would stay the one author of a defect, and the agent amending its documents stays refused
  ([the-model-compiles-a-routine-and-is-called-back-at-its-edges](/decisions/the-model-compiles-a-routine-and-is-called-back-at-its-edges.md));
  but a sensor gone strange is doubted from the moment it is noticed, and a doubt that waits for a restart
  is a dose sized from a number nobody believes. The sovereign chose the word that counts at once.

# Seams left open

- **A percept already revised is not revised again** when a report arrives; the next reading carries it.
- **Nothing says who reported a defect** to a reader of the percept: the statement keeps `speech:saidBy`, and
  the defect graph is derived from it, a provenance step away.

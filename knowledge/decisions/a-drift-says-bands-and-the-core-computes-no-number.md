# The claim

**The drift is the mechanism.** A [drift](/domain/effect.md) is a package's rule the kernel
runs over `$elapsed`, at every fork of a pass and at the root of one that stands ahead of
the present, and `orexis:crossesAfter` beside it answers WHEN a reading leaves its band. That
stays. It is the package's arithmetic in the package's text, run where the search needs it,
which is the shape everything else here has: the desire owns the term and the package owns
the measure. Replacing it with predictions computed ahead and stored — a predictor service
writing graphs at horizons, an overlay by key at each node — was proposed the same afternoon
and is refused below: it would store what the search can compute at the instant it needs,
beside a second machinery for reading it.

**A prediction is bands, and the package's rule says which.** What crossed the package
boundary as a NUMBER is what is wrong.
[deliberation-is-on-triples-and-a-number-is-not-special](/decisions/deliberation-is-on-triples-and-a-number-is-not-special.md)
ruled that the core compares triples and interprets no literal, and the width built for
#631 broke it twice in one day: `orexis:spreadsBy` handed the kernel a width, and sensing
summed it with `sensing:noise` and mapped a centre onto bands by the region's edges. The
rule's job is to type the predicted reading with every band it may be in at that horizon —
the rate, the spread the world states beside it and the instrument's noise inside its own
text, the band definitions' own facets for the edges — and to emit no number the core reads.
A world stating no spread and no noise gets the one band the rate reaches; the far horizon
gets every band. `sensing:noise` and `water:driesPerDaySpread` stay as facts the rule reads;
`spreadsBy` goes.

**Three readers, no arithmetic.**

- **The search.** A predicted reading typed with the region band and the one below reads UNMET
  under the stake's shape, so a plan is asked for wherever the region MAY be left — the safe
  direction, with no kernel line knowing a rate. The crossing
  ([#633](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/633)) is `crossesAfter`'s to say the
  same way, from inside the rule.
- **The expected next observation** ([prediction](/domain/prediction.md)). Sensing runs the
  drifts at the window's far end, as the planner runs them at a fork, and records the bands the
  predicted reading carries as what the next reading `sensing:mayBe`. Its centre-and-width
  arithmetic is retired.
- **The keeper's watch.** A step's prediction is the band its effect declared (#579), and the
  world answers with a reading that IS that band: membership, one query over the reading's
  types, not a shape with a number in it. The answering shape built per step — a SHACL node
  shape with `sh:minInclusive` and `sh:maxInclusive` on a value, compiled and held on the
  step — and the watch on a number both retire; a number handed in is held to the band it
  falls in, and the number stays in the residual the reviewer reads. The shape road had a
  blind spot the suite found the day the expectation stood beside the readings: a node stating
  no value passes a constraint on a value vacuously.

**One comparison at arrival is every verdict.** When a reading arrives, sensing looks once at
whether it is in the bands the expected next observation named. In them: absorbed, no pass,
and a standing step whose landing has come and whose band it is is MET. Outside them: a
surprise, a pass that names it, and a standing step past its landing whose band it is not is
UNMET. The window closing with none: missed, the reading in hand stale, the freshness want
awake. A standing step enters the expectation through the keeper — `orexis:predicted`, told
when a watch opens and closes: the step, its band, its landing — and sensing types the
expectation past the landing with the step's band, carried by the drift after it. The cone
already identifies the present by the same look (#576).

# What was refused

- **A predictor service and prediction graphs in place of the drift.** Stored predictions
  are the drift's answers at horizons somebody chose in advance, and the search stands at
  instants nobody chose; the overlay by key at each node is a second reader beside the one
  that runs the rule. The drift computes at the instant asked and stores nothing.
- **A width as a kernel word.** `orexis:spreadsBy` gave the kernel a number to add; the
  bands the rule types the reading with are what it may add. Retired.
- **Arithmetic in sensing.** Centre plus and minus width against a region's edges is the
  domain's arithmetic in the wrong package; sensing keeps the window and copies the bands.
- **The watch on a number**, and the shape that held it. Bands, and membership.
- **Hysteresis in time** ([#615](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/615)). A
  reading at 11.98 against a region starting at 12, with the instrument's noise stated, is
  predicted in both bands and the flip is absorbed; the width does the dwell's work and
  invents nothing. A world stating no noise flips, and then the flip is a surprise, honestly.
  Closed on this record.

# What stays, and why

The drift and its crossing; effects, landings, costs and availability; the residual, written
as two numbers for the reviewer and compared by the review's own rules; suspicion; the
remembered plan; every hold that is not a wait for a reading; the periods door and the
forecast. `sensing:noise` and `water:driesPerDaySpread` as facts.

# The word

"Expectation" is the expected next observation, sensing's. The keeper's row on a step is its
**watch**, its stake in the expectation from the step's landing. `OpenExpectation` and
`open_expectations` are the old rendering's names and go with it.

# What is built, in the order it is built

1. **The drift says bands** ([#642](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/642)):
   the water, climate and market drifts type the predicted reading with every band it may be
   in at `$elapsed`, from their rate, their spread and the instrument's noise, in their own
   texts; `orexis:spreadsBy` and sensing's width arithmetic retire, and the expected next
   observation copies the drift's bands. The search reads the set through the shapes it
   already has, which is the safe direction of #633.
2. **The keeper tells sensing the intended branch, and the verdict comes from the comparison**
   ([#639](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/639)): `orexis:predicted`, the
   watch by band membership, the answering shape and the watch on a number retired.
3. **The reviser wakes on a surprise and a missed window, and on nothing else**
   ([#632](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/632)).
4. **The word** ([#640](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/640)).

# Seams left open

- **A drift after a step inside a pass** still runs from the step's declared band, as it does
  today; a step's band is one band, and the drift widens it from there.
- **Two steps landing inside one window.** The expectation past the last step's landing is
  the last step's band; a plan whose steps land closer than the cadence is judged by its
  last step.
- **A reading between the taking and the landing** is compared with the do-nothing branch
  and says nothing of the step; a world that answers early is absorbed.
- **Resuming from the branch point** ([#527](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/527)):
  a surprise now names the step and the band it failed; whether keeping the frontier pays
  is still that issue's measurement.

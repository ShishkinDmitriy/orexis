# What it is

An **aim** is where in the [region](/domain/region.md) this agent wants the property to sit. The
region says 0.10 to 0.30; the aim says 0.18, *dry-side of centre, deliberately*, because the
survival ceiling is a rot cliff and the floor is nearly free.

**It is the agent's**, and it is the only part of a desire that is. The region arrives deduced
from what the subject states; the aim is a judgement made inside it.

# A structure, not a triple

`ag:aims` carries a node with the property and the value, not a bare number — which is why an
amendment endowing an agent with an aim endows a whole structure, and why the old `hasTarget`
could not simply be renamed into it.

That shape is what lets one agent want things in several properties without anything special
happening.

# Genesis writes one, and does not fix it

An aim is the clearest case of the thing [review](/domain/review.md) exists for — a setting an
agent may re-pick inside room it was given — and that page has the argument.

The consequence for whoever authors a world: **getting the aim right matters much less than
stating a range inside which being wrong is survivable.**

# It moves; the region does not

The asymmetry is the whole architecture in one line. An agent may move its aim, because an aim is
a preference about its own conduct. It may **never** move the region, because a region is what its
subject needs and the agent is not the authority on that.

A revision that moved a region would be an agent deciding what a plant requires. That is refused
structurally: the constraint graph is public, recomputed from the ratified files on every start,
and a review's write boundary is the agent's own beliefs.

# Where it lives, and why that is wrong today

An aim is a **want**, and it sits in the beliefs graph beside cadences and ceilings, which are
settings. The modality axis says a graph's type is what its contents assert, and those are not the
same assertion — so this is the one place the split is violated, and it is filed rather than
excused. Under a store per modality the triple could not be written at all.

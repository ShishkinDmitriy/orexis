# What it is

An agent that is a **buyer in one market and a seller in another, with stock between them**.
Not a mechanism and not an institution: a participant, whose inventory glues an N-to-1 and a
1-to-M market together where no collective venue could be ruled by anyone
([the-market-has-no-governor](/decisions/the-market-has-no-governor.md)). The shipped
[supplier](/domain/supplier.md) is one: it bids for refill litres at the city's venue and
sells litres to the plants at its own, the barrel between them.

# The stock is the decoupling

The dealer's upstream claim is redeemed **by the dealer itself** — the mains' valve doses its
own barrel — and its downstream venue mints **fresh claims against its own stock**. The two
markets never learn of each other; no claim crosses; the plan connecting them lives in one
agent's head (and since #359 found by its search: acquire upstream, then offer downstream). This
is what inventory *is*: absorbing the mismatch in time, quantity and price between the buy
side and the sell side. The pass-through dealer — no vessel, claim endorsed to a beneficiary
who is not the winner — is a designed seam with its four-signature chain recorded, built by
nobody because nothing needs it.

# What keeps it honest

No referee — four standing facts: **entry is one triple** (whoever holds a source and states
`matchesBy` has opened shop, so the next dealer costs a ratification, not a licence);
**terms precede play, in public** (the matching travels with every offer); **the ledger sees
the spread** (bought-at and sold-at are signed events — the margin is a subtraction the
sovereign can perform); and **the uncontested round clears at cost**
([uniform price](/domain/bid-matching.md)). Its stock is honest too: rounds are sized by the
vessel's own witness, a LOW nobody can serve is DEFERRED as an owed round in the intention
ledger (#206), and phantom water is a closed chapter.

# What grants it

The dealer premise is **levers that compose** — acting for a source it offers, refillable
from a source another offers — and it is what granted the retired deliberation package's `Planning`: depth-2
deliberation is meaningful for exactly this shape, and for nothing shipped besides it.

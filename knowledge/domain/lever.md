# What it is

A **lever** is the particular thing an act is performed *through*. The gardener's pump; fern's
moisture probe; the venue the city hosts. It is the `via` column of an
[affordance](/domain/affordance.md) row, and unlike the [means](/domain/means.md) beside it, a
lever is always an **instance** — so no code may name one, and every lever an agent has is
discovered by walking the world graph from its own id.

# Why it is a separate word from the means

Because one means reaches many levers, and the difference is what makes the menu longer than the
list of verbs. A supplier that actuates three pots holds one means and three levers, and it can
lose one of them without losing the ability. Fern looks through a moisture probe and a
thermometer: one means, two levers, two rows, two properties.

The reverse never happens — a lever belongs to one kind of act. A valve is not something you can
`Observe` through.

# A lever's absence is what makes the menu honest

This is the property worth the word. **A row exists because every hop of its walk holds**, and the
lever is the hop most likely to stop holding: a valve unplumbed from a pot, a probe that watches
a different subject, a venue no longer plumbed to your source. When that happens the row simply
is not derived, and the agent stops proposing an act it cannot take — with nothing edited and
nothing to forget to edit.

That is the same argument [affordance](/domain/affordance.md) makes about rows never being
stored, seen from the other end: the reason a stored row is dangerous is precisely that a lever
can go away underneath it.

# What it is not

**Not the actuator.** An `actuation:Valve` is a device; a lever is the ROLE that device plays in
one row. The same valve is a lever for the agent that holds it and no lever at all for the agent
next to it, and nothing about the device changes between those two facts.

**Not a capability.** Holding a lever and being able to use it are different things: a capability
is granted by a premise — for actuation, by the wiring that gives the agent a valve at all — and
the lever is the individual that premise found. See
[capability-packages](/decisions/capability-packages.md).

# What it is

The search plans on templates. For facts it always did; for numbers the record
[a-predicted-number-is-an-interval](/decisions/a-predicted-number-is-an-interval.md) says why
a point was wrong and what replaces it. A number in a possible world is a pair of ends.

# What is built (#556)

- **An effect declares two bounds.** The dosing, acquiring and serving rules bind the reading
  they expect and, beside it, what it is at least and at most: the movement widened by
  `actuation:tolerance` or `market:tolerance` — the agent's own pick, or the capability's
  default — read by the RULE from the agent's beliefs, where the arithmetic is. A standing
  reading that already has a width is read with its ends, so the width grows along a chain.
- **Sensing's words carry it.** A predicted observation states `sensing:atLeast` and
  `sensing:atMost` beside `sosa:hasSimpleResult`; the kernel's `orexis:lowEndOf` and
  `orexis:highEndOf` say which carried value they bound, and the signature folds the three
  into one fact whose value is the pair. A narrower interval at the same midpoint is a new
  world; an observed reading has no ends and is a point.
- **The step carries the pair and the keeper holds the world to it.** The answering shape is
  the interval, drawn by sensing from the two ends the step states; the actor passes nothing.
  The residual records the midpoint, so review reads what it always read.
- **A present inside the interval is the world the step predicted**, exactly: the cone
  re-roots there with its subtree whole ([identification](/domain/identification.md)).
- **A look carries a standing width through unchanged.** What it narrows to is the
  instrument's grain, which is #558's to say; until then a look after a dose nets to nothing.

# Not built yet

- **Met** reads the midpoint; certainly met — the interval inside the [region](/domain/region.md)
  — is [#557](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/557), with the law refusing a
  step whose interval can cross the bound, the estimate at the far end, the cost at the cheap
  end, and a dose as an interval of litres the actor picks inside. This is not the alarm
  [band](/domain/band.md) a board keeps.
- **A reading widened by its age**, starting at the sensor's grain and unbounded past the
  horizon, is [#558](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/558).

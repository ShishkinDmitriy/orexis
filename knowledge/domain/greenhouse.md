# What it is

A bed, a pump, a heater, and a want that asks for both properties at once. Beside hanoi and the
courier it is a planning domain with no Python; unlike them it uses the shipped capabilities —
[sensing](/domain/sensing.md) reads its two properties, [actuation](/domain/actuation.md)'s
Dosing waters it — and adds exactly one [action](/domain/action.md) of its own, `greenhouse:Heating`.

The heater is **knowledge-only**: nothing takes it, so it is planned and never executed, as
hanoi's Move is. What this world exists to measure is the search.

# What it is the first of

- **A want about several things.** Its desire names two `orexis:about`, so the menu offers a row
  per property and a single want reaches two levers. Every want before it named one, and the
  afforder returned one about per want because nothing needed two.
- **A shape about several properties.** Its met-test says no reading of the soil is a
  [band](/domain/band.md) below the region, and no reading of the air is either. The gate that
  holds a shape to naming its property was widened from exactly one to one or more.
- **A plan with a step from each half.** A dose and a heating, neither serving the other's
  property.

# The knob, and what it shows

`greenhouse:driesTheSoil` on the heater is one triple, and the two regimes are the same world
one fact apart.

- **Off**, warming writes only the air's reading. The halves touch nothing of each other, and
  the plan waters and then warms.
- **On**, warming also writes the soil's reading, below its region. A plan that watered and then
  warmed would have undone its own dose — so the search puts the heating FIRST. Nothing declares
  an order and nothing knows the word evaporation: the coupling is in the rules, and the search
  reads it.

# What it measured, and what it did not

The coupling is real and the [scope](/domain/scope.md) computation cannot see it. A scope is a
set of predicates, and both of this bed's readings carry the same predicates, so warming and
dosing write the same words in both regimes: ONE scope either way. Telling these two halves
apart needs a scope over variables, a subject and a property together. That is why one cone per
scope is still unbuilt, and this world is the case that will measure it when it is.

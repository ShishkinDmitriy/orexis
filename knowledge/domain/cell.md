# What it is

Every number a rule compares a property against is a boundary the world can be on either
side of, and between two boundaries the world cannot behave differently, since nothing reads
the difference. The boundaries sorted cut the property's line into cells, written as their
bounds — the low one inclusive, the high one exclusive, either end open. Moisture in the
loner world has the region's two bounds and the envelope's two: below the floor, dry, in
region, wet, past the ceiling. A pot at 0.25 and a pot at 0.27 are one world; so are 0.30 and
0.31, which used to be the drift that killed a plant's cone.

# Where the thresholds come from

Read, never authored: the bounds of every shape that constrains a reading of the property —
a want's [region](/domain/region.md), the survival envelope, a law — and the constants a
select compares the reading's variable against, walked by the parser [relevance](/domain/relevance.md)
uses. A property some select compares against a VARIABLE, which the parser cannot resolve to a
number, stays point-valued, and that is the discipline relevance keeps applied to values: a
boundary the parser cannot see would collapse two worlds a rule tells apart, quietly. The
butt's level, compared against the litres a serve needs, is such a property today.

# Where it is used, and where it is not

A [reading](/domain/reading.md) is matched by its cell where a present is looked up among the
kept worlds ([identification](/domain/identification.md)) and where a step states its
[premises](/domain/precondition.md), so a remembered plan applies to the dry cell rather than
to 0.23. It is NOT how the search tells its own worlds apart: a dose that moves the pot within
its cell is progress toward the boundary, and steering to the aim inside the region is work,
so the cone's diffs stay by number and cycle detection sees the number. Deliberation reasons
over cells where it compares worlds to the present; progression keeps the numbers — the actor
sizes the dose against the live reading, the keeper holds the world to the step's predicted
number.

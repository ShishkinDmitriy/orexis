# What it is

`progression:Act`. The record of a taking: `progression:of` the [step](/domain/step.md), `progression:takenAt`,
and `progression:taken` — whether an actor carried it out or none could now, the step standing for
the next tick. Nothing planned lives here: planning's noun is the step, and this one says that
one of them was carried out (the sovereign's ruling, 2026-09-02).

# Where it appears

- written by `execution.carry_out`, on the ledger beside the step, each time a step is handed
  to its actors
- read back as the history of an instance: what was attempted, when, and by the intention's
  verdict beside it, what the world made of it

# What it is not

**Not the [step](/domain/step.md).** The step carries the lever, the quantity, the window and the
prediction; the act carries the fact of the attempt.

**Not the [intention](/domain/intention.md).** The intention is the commitment to a want, with
its own adoption, resolution and verdict; the act is one attempt at one of its steps.

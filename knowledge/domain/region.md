# What it is

A **region** is the range a subject states it needs a property to hold inside — a Zamioculcas
between 0.10 and 0.30 of saturation. Beside every region sits an **envelope**: the range outside
which the subject does not merely sit badly but *ends*.

An agent holds one per property, and **which properties is not its choice** — nor the sovereign's
to write down. It is whatever the thing the agent acts for states a range for.

# It is deduced, never authored

Nothing writes a region. It is computed at genesis by **intersecting every operating range that
applies** to the subject — the highest floor anyone states, the lowest ceiling — with the survival
ranges gathered the same way to make the envelope.

Two kinds of bearer contribute: the **subject** itself, through its own range or its species'; and
any instrument that watches it. **A region an agent cannot witness itself inside is not one it can
hold.**

The property is bound from the *subject's* conditions. **An instrument may narrow a region and may
never create one** — a thermometer rated 0–50 °C states a fact about the thermometer, not an
ambition for the pot.

# It is not an aim

A region is a **range**; an [aim](/domain/aim.md) is a point inside it. The region is stated by
the world and the agent may not move it; the aim is the agent's own pick. Collapsing the two is
the oldest mistake in this design — a desire that was three decimals in a beliefs file, a target
and two edges, denominated in one property and unable to want anything else.

# It is asked of a modality, not of a graph by name

The result lives in the desire modality — a store of the mind's own, **asked as a whole rather
than by any graph's name** ([a-store-is-a-modality](/decisions/a-store-is-a-modality.md)),
because desire may have more than one source and a reader must not have to know how many. The
sovereign's ratified mandate, the regions deduced from a subject's ranges, and the obligations received
on a peer's claim are three sources and one question.

It is **recomputed on every start**, because it is a function of the ratified files: a
plant whose range is amended must not leave an agent holding the old region.

# A violation names its side

A region is several property [shapes](/domain/shape.md) rather than one — not-looked, below,
above — because a violation has a *side*, and a reader needs to know which. Which edge was crossed
travels with the constraint instead of being reconstructed by whoever caught it, and the message
is baked at derivation time, since a shape minted per (agent, property) already knows both edges.

# In Agent 0.2.0, a range is SSN-System's and nothing is intersected

`agent/sensing/` reads a range as the world states it — `ssn-system:hasOperatingRange` and
`ssn-system:hasSurvivalRange` on the subject or on an instrument that monitors it, a condition
`ssn:forProperty` the property with `schema:minValue` and `schema:maxValue` — and mints
nothing (#785). The two readers of a range are sensing itself, for where a predicted number
crosses a bound, and the three rules
sensing registers, which conclude `sensing:below`, `sensing:inside` or `sensing:above` each
range as a [revision](/domain/revision.md) of an observation. The intersection this page
describes above is the 0.1.0 tree's; in 0.2.0 it moves to the desire's met-test, which asks
about the ranges it means.

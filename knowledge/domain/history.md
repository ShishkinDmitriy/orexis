# What it is

When [identification](/domain/identification.md) moves the root of the
[imaginarium](/domain/imaginarium.md) to the child the present matched, the old root is not
discarded. It is the newest entry of history: the present that was, the [act](/domain/act.md)
that was taken there, and the diff between that present and this one — what actually happened,
beside what the step predicted. Kept as diffs, it costs a few triples a step; kept as snapshots
it would cost a world a step, and that was refused.

# What bounds it

History is a ring. Nothing points into it, and whatever must outlive the window copies what it
needs at commit time, which is the discipline
[the-mind-is-six-graphs](/decisions/the-mind-is-six-graphs.md) set for it. What reads it is the
lifting of a [remembered plan](/domain/remembered-plan.md), the residuals a
[review](/domain/review.md) learns from, and suspicion.

# Not built yet

Nothing writes the act, the observed diff is one number, and the history graph class has no
instance. The issue is [#555](https://github.com/ShishkinDmitriy/orexis/issues/555).

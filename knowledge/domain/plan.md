# What it is

The [planner](/domain/planner.md)'s answer to one [desire](/domain/desire.md). Its steps are
[steps](/domain/step.md), in the order they are to be taken, and each carries what the search
predicted taking it would reach and, since #550, its [precondition](/domain/precondition.md)
there. Beside the steps: the outcome — satisfied, or one of the silences the search may return
instead of steps — the urgency now and after, the cost it was scored to spend, and the candidate
of the root's menu it came through, which the trace names.

A plan with no steps is an answer, and the outcome says which one: nothing to do, nothing
possible, a menu the search could not fully simulate, a budget spent.

# What happens to one

- An [intention](/domain/intention.md) commits to its steps and stands at one; the plan itself is
  not written anywhere.
- The world verifies each step when it is taken, and a step that fails drops the tail.
- One that worked is kept as a [remembered plan](/domain/remembered-plan.md), keyed by the
  regression of its steps' preconditions, and may be lifted to a [method](/domain/method.md).

# What it is not

**Not a chain, a path or a route.** It is steps, and the bundle says steps; the search's
own path through its worlds is the cone's, not the plan's.

**Not history.** Nothing in it has happened. What happened is [acts](/domain/act.md).

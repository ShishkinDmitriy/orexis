# What happens in one

Three things, in an order that is the whole point.

**Derive.** `derive_wants` judges every desire this agent holds, at the present and at each
instant a prediction reaches, and mints or withdraws accordingly. Everything downstream reads
its output, so nothing downstream judges again — which is why no row has to be corrected by a
second met-test.

**Weigh.** The wants that stand are collected with whatever the packages holding their stakes
say about them. Nothing is ranked: the order a pass takes them in decides which is searched
first and nothing else.

**Mark.** Each want that may be acted on is noted for the thread that searches. Nothing is
planned here, because a pass begins on the reactive loop and a search may take seconds.

# Considering is not pursuing

They are a pass apart, and the gap between them is where the interesting cases live.

A want nobody may act on yet — a debt whose holder has not asked — is **considered** every
pass and **pursued** never, because a search over it would decide nothing. A want a plan is
already walking is considered too, and pursuing it again is absorbed rather than repeated. So
the set the agent weighs is wider than the set it acts on, deliberately, and a reader asking
*what is this agent doing* wants the second while a reader asking *what does it hold* wants
the first.

# Who begins one

The container, on the agent's patience — [the layer that waits does the waiting](/decisions/layered-by-timescale-and-interruptibility.md).
Deliberation once kept a timer of its own whose landing reached back into the container for
what to weigh, which left no single place a pass began; the wait is the container's now and
what it calls is this.

A reading arriving, a claim landing or a round opening starts one too, scoped to the want it
concerns. Same pass, same order, one want.

# What it is not

- **Not the search.** What runs here decides nothing about which move to take; that is the
  [deliberator](/domain/deliberator.md)'s, on a thread of its own.
- **Not a store.** Nothing writes down what a pass weighed. The wants are read from the graphs
  that hold them and what the packages add is made fresh, because an answer about a situation
  is wrong by the next pass.

# orexis-transport-mqtt

The MQTT member of the transport family: readings and peers' documents in, commands and the agent's documents out.

- **Loaded for:** no role of its own. It is loaded where a loaded role needs bytes and the society wires a broker.
- **Words:** `ontology.ttl` (`mqtt:`), speaking MQTT4SSN. **Dictionary:** `knowledge/domain/transport/transport.md`.
- **Needs:** where each broker it is connected to answers, from the world's derived deployment, and the agent's credentials, from its environment and `secrets/<agent>/`.

## What starts it

| Heard | Said by | Starts |
|---|---|---|
| a message on a subscribed topic (an arrival) | the broker | left with each row listening on a channel the topic is of: sensing for a sensor's reading, speech for a peer's document on the agent's own topic or a chat's words on its topic |

It holds the agent running, since what it senses goes on arriving whatever is wanted.

## Activities

None: what it is handed and sent is a row's above, which listens and sends through it.
